THE OPERATING GAP

Teams can introduce agents faster than they can govern them.

When an agent prepares a release note, routes a support action, changes a record, or calls a connected tool, someone should be able to answer: who owned it, what was allowed, who approved it, and what evidence closed it?

CONTROL CHECKLIST

  1. Register the agent and accountable owner.
  2. State the proposed action and allowed scope.
  3. Assign a risk tier and human decision point.
  4. Require evidence before recording the outcome.
